\begin{methodbox}
\textbf{Reusable five-step procedure:} (1) identify the model and
basis; (2) state operator/phase conventions; (3) calculate symbolically
before substituting numbers; (4) normalise and check units, parity,
state-count or probability; (5) interpret what is measured physically.
\end{methodbox}
