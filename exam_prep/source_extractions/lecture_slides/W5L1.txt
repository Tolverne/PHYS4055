====== PAGE 31 ======
Summary


 • An open-system evolution results from a unitary system-environment
    interaction followed by tracing over the unobserved environment.

 • Many weak environmental interactions can often be represented by a single
    effective interaction, simplifying the description of an optical channel.

 •  Amplitude damping exchanges energy and changes photon-number
    populations, while phase damping preserves photon number but suppresses
   coherence and phase-sensitive observables.
