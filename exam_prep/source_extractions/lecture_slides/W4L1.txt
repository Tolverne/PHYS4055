 Photon polarisation – qubit operations

         In order to implement single qubit unitary operations on polarisation encoded qubits, we
       need to manipulate the polarisation of light.

     We can do this using wave plates. Waveplates are made from birefringent materials
        which have slightly different indices of refraction for light polarized in different orientations.
       They delay orthogonal polarization differently, so they recombine with a different phase.
