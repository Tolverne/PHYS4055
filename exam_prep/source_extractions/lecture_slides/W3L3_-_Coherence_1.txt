====== PAGE 8 ======
Photon arrival statistics

Photon bunching
                                       g(2)(0) > 1

 Photons are more likely to arrive close together than for independent arrivals.
 Example: thermal light  g(2)(0) = 2

 Independent arrivals
                                       g(2)(0) = 1
 Detecting one photon does not affect the likelihood of detecting another.
 Example: coherent light.


Photon antibunching
                                       g(2)(0) < 1

Photons are less likely to arrive close together than for independent arrivals.
Example: an ideal single-photon state g(2)(0) = 0


                       Antibunching is a signature of nonclassical light.
