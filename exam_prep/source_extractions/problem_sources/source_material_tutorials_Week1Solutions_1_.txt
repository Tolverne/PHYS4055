                              Question 2: Coherent states as eigenstates


 A coherent state is defined by

                                      |α⟩= ˆD(α)|0⟩,                                              (31)

where |0⟩is the vacuum state.
  Using the displacement-operator identity

                                                    ˆD†(α)ˆa ˆD(α) = ˆa + α,                                           (32)

show that |α⟩is an eigenstate of the annihilation operator ˆa.



                                             Worked solution


 We begin with the action of the annihilation operator on the coherent state:

                                             ˆa|α⟩= ˆa ˆD(α)|0⟩.                                             (33)

 We insert the identity operator in the form

                                                                       ˆI = ˆD(α) ˆD†(α).                                             (34)

  Therefore,

                                        ˆa|α⟩= ˆD(α) ˆD†(α)ˆa ˆD(α)|0⟩.                                       (35)

 We now use

                                                    ˆD†(α)ˆa ˆD(α) = ˆa + α.                                           (36)

  This gives

                                          ˆa|α⟩= ˆD(α) (ˆa + α) |0⟩.                                          (37)

  Expanding the right-hand side gives

                                        ˆa|α⟩= ˆD(α)ˆa|0⟩+ α ˆD(α)|0⟩.                                       (38)

  The annihilation operator acting on the vacuum gives

                                                   ˆa|0⟩= 0.                                                 (39)

  Therefore,

                                                 ˆD(α)ˆa|0⟩= 0.                                               (40)

  The remaining term is

                                             ˆa|α⟩= α ˆD(α)|0⟩.                                             (41)

  Using

                                             ˆD(α)|0⟩= |α⟩,                                              (42)

we obtain

                                               ˆa|α⟩= α|α⟩.                                               (43)

  Therefore, the coherent state |α⟩is an eigenstate of the annihilation operator ˆa, with eigenvalue α.

                                               ˆa|α⟩= α|α⟩.                                               (50)

  Using the coherent-state eigenvalue equation gives

                                              ⟨α|ˆa|α⟩= α⟨α|α⟩.                                             (57)

                                         ⟨α|α⟩= 1.                                                (58)

                               α∗= |α|e−iθ.                                               (63)

                                              αeiϕ = |α|ei(ϕ+θ).                                             (64)

                                          (

                                                    eix + e−ix = 2 cos(x),                                           (67)
