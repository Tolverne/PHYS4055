## Transcript coverage

The 2026 transcript files have numeric suffixes corresponding to **download order, not a verified chronological lecture index**. Checked transcript themes include quadratures/coherent states (unsuffixed), detection (1), quasiprobabilities and squeezing (2), coherence (3), photonic qubits/entanglement (4), quantum communication (5), optical channels (6), conditional optics (7), collective light–matter interaction (8), the atomic switch-over (9/10), fine/hyperfine and angular-momentum methods (11–15), transitions/cooling (16), precision/symmetry applications (17–18), and exam preparation (19). Suffixes (9) and (10) appear identical: do not count as separate substantive lectures.

## Accuracy checks and caveats

- Optics slides use `x=a+a†`, `p=i(a†−a)` (vacuum variance 1). Other references differ by factors of `sqrt(2)`.
- Beam splitters have several valid phase conventions; transforms in the booklet use a self-consistent real unitary. The signal and ancilla order must be declared.
- Week 8 and Week 9 handwritten equations are incompletely extractable; the overview uses standard expressions and points back to source sections.
- Week 10 discusses one- and two-level laser dynamics, selection rules, optical pumping, optical molasses, MOT and laser cooling; Week 12 discusses fundamental parity/CP tests and EDMs; Week 13 discusses cesium and optical clocks.
- An apparent Week 13 slide typo writes a cesium clock transition as `F=3,mF=0 -> F=3,mF=0`. The physical clock transition is `F=3,mF=0 -> F=4,mF=0`.
- The original PDF for every exam question should still be consulted; this overview is not presented as a fully audited solution book.
