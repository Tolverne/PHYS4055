Question 1


(10 marks)


In this question, we look at the hyperfine splitting of levels, which occurs due to the interaction of
atomic electrons with the nucleus.

We will consider the atom with electronic term 2P1/2 and nuclear spin I = 5/2.


  (a) (1 mark) What are the possible values of the total atomic angular momentum F, where F =
      I + J, with I and J the nuclear spin and electronic angular momentum?

 (b) (2 marks) The Hamiltonian for the magnetic hyperfine interaction may be expressed as the
      scalar product of the nuclear spin and electronic angular momentum, H = AI · J, where A is
     the magnetic hyperfine constant.

    Show that the hyperfine energy shift is given by

                      A
                 ∆E =   F(F + 1) −I(I + 1) −J(J + 1)   ,                      (1)
                                 2

     showing all working.

  (c) (2 marks) Hence, find an expression for the energy difference between hyperfine levels for the
     considered term, and sketch an energy-level diagram.

 (d) (3 marks) Consider that a weak magnetic field is applied to the atom.

         (i) (1 mark) Show how your energy-level diagram from (c) is modified in this field, justifying
          your answer.

         (ii) (1 mark) How many levels of different energy are there? Explain your result.

        (iii) (1 mark) Describe, with the aid of a diagram, the pattern of the energy-level splitting if
