(d) Decoding eﬀiciency

Two of the four Bell states can be identified unambiguously. For four equally likely messages,
the success probability is

                                            2

                                            4
                                            1
                           = 2.                                   (51)

Thus, the standard passive linear-optical Bell-state measurement succeeds with probability
50%.
The other 50% of the events correspond to |Φ+⟩or |Φ−⟩, which cannot be distinguished.
Consequently, this measurement alone cannot implement deterministic dense coding.
