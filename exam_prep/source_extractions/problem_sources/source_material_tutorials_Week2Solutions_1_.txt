                       A.  Question 2: Energy conservation at a beam splitter


  Consider a lossless beam splitter acting on two optical modes. The input and output mode operators are related
by
                          √
                                                                                  ˆa′ = √η ˆa −  1 −η ˆb,

and
                       √
                                                                                          ˆb′ =   1 −η ˆa + √η ˆb,

where 0 ≤η ≤1 is the beam-splitter transmissivity.
  The photon-number operators of the two output modes are

                                                     ˆNa′ = ˆa′†ˆa′,      ˆNb′ = ˆb′†ˆb′.
   1. Write down the transformed creation operators ˆa′† and ˆb′†.
   2. Calculate ˆNa′ explicitly in terms of the input-mode operators ˆa, ˆa†, ˆb, and ˆb†.
   3. Similarly, calculate ˆNb′ in terms of the input-mode operators.

   4. By adding your results, show that

                                                            ˆNa′ + ˆNb′ = ˆNa + ˆNb,

     where
                                                    ˆNa = ˆa†ˆa,      ˆNb = ˆb†ˆb.

Solution

   1. Taking the Hermitian conjugate of the output-mode operators gives
                            √
                                                                            ˆa′† = √η ˆa† −  1 −η ˆb†,

     and
                        √
                                                                                 ˆb′† =   1 −η ˆa† + √η ˆb†.

   2. The photon-number operator for output mode a′ is
                                                                  ˆNa′ = ˆa′†ˆa′.

                                    B.  Question 3: Partial trace


 A single photon passes through a balanced beam splitter, producing the two-mode state

                                             1
                                           |ψ⟩ab = √  (|1⟩a|0⟩b + |0⟩a|1⟩b) .
                                              2

   1. Write down the two-mode density operator

                                                                        ˆρab = |ψ⟩ab⟨ψ|.

   2. Suppose that we only have access to mode a. Calculate its reduced density operator by tracing over mode b:

                   ∑1
                                                               ˆρa = Trb(ˆρab) =     b⟨n|ˆρab|n⟩b.
                                                      n=0

   3. What is the probability of detecting zero photons in mode a? What is the probability of detecting one photon?

   4. Is the reduced state ˆρa pure or mixed?

   1. The two-mode density operator is

                                                                        ˆρab = |ψ⟩ab⟨ψ|.
