====== PAGE 4 ======
Quasi-probability distributions
Overview


                   In this lecture, we will look at three different quasi-probability distributions used in
              quantum optics:

                                         Husimi Q function

                                     Glauber Sudarshan P function

                                         Wigner function

                       •   Useful for visualising quantum states and their evolution

                       •  Each of the Q, P and W functions have their own advantages and disadvantages

                       •  The Q, P and W functions are related to each other

                       •  Why are they called quasi-probability distributions?

                –  Could be negative or ill-behaved
