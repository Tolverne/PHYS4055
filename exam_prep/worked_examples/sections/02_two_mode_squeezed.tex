
\section{QO-02: Two-mode squeezed vacuum, correlations and heralding}
\source{2026 Optics Assignment 1, Question 1; released Assignment 1 solutions.}
\subsection*{Given state and first checks}
Let \(\lambda=\tanh r\), \(0\le\lambda<1\), and
\[
\ket\Psi=\sqrt{1-\lambda^2}
\sum_{n=0}^\infty\lambda^n\ket n_s\ket n_i .
\]
Normalisation follows from
\((1-\lambda^2)\sum_{n=0}^\infty(\lambda^2)^n=1\).
Every pair has identical signal and idler photon number.

\subsection*{Step 1: trace out the idler}
Expand \(\rho_{si}=\ket\Psi\bra\Psi\):
\[
\rho_{si}=(1-\lambda^2)\sum_{m,n}
\lambda^{m+n}\ket m_s\bra n_s\otimes\ket m_i\bra n_i.
\]
The idler trace gives
\(\operatorname{Tr}_i(\ket m\bra n)=\delta_{mn}\):
\[
\boxed{\rho_s=(1-\lambda^2)\sum_{n=0}^\infty
\lambda^{2n}\ket n\bra n}.
\]
This is thermal photon statistics: set \(q=\lambda^2\) so
\(p_n=(1-q)q^n\). Differentiating the geometric series,
\[
\bar n=(1-q)\sum_n nq^n=\frac{q}{1-q}
=\boxed{\frac{\lambda^2}{1-\lambda^2}=\sinh^2r}.
\]
The \emph{joint} state is pure, but either subsystem is mixed.

\subsection*{Step 2: calculate the signal--idler correlation}
Because \(n_s=n_i=n\) in every term,
\[
\langle\hat n_s\hat n_i\rangle=\langle n^2\rangle,\qquad
\langle n^2\rangle=(\Delta n)^2+\bar n^2
=(\bar n+\bar n^2)+\bar n^2.
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{g_{si}^{(2)}(0)=
\frac{\langle\hat n_s\hat n_i\rangle}{\bar n^2}
=2+\frac{1}{\bar n}}.
\]
For example \(\lambda=0.2\) gives
\(\bar n=1/24\simeq0.0417\) and \(g_{si}^{(2)}=26\).

\subsection*{Step 3: explain heralding}
At low gain \(\lambda\ll1\),
\(\ket\Psi\simeq\ket{0,0}+\lambda\ket{1,1}+O(\lambda^2)\).
Conditioning on detecting exactly one idler photon projects
the signal onto \(\ket1\). A threshold detector with nonzero
multi-pair probability does not guarantee a perfect \(\ket1\)
at higher gain.
\checkmethod{A reduced thermal state does \emph{not} contradict
strong pairwise correlations: the traced-out idler held the
information about the signal photon number.}
