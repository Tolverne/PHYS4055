====== PAGE 91 ======
Doppler shift

■  Atoms absorb radiation at frequency ω in the rest frame
■  Say we have an atom moving at velocity v:

■    It will see different frequency radiation, depending on whether it’s moving towards or
   away from its source.
■  That means we need to account for the Doppler shift when selecting the laser frequency
