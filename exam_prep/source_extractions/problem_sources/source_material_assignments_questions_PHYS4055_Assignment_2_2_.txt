sends Bob a new photon prepared according to her measurement outcome.
The quantum bit error rate (QBER) is the fraction of sifted-key bits for which Bob’s bit
differs from Alice’s bit:

                                                        Nsift
Because the QBER is calculated using the sifted key, Alice and Bob are already known to
have used the same basis. Calculate the QBER caused by Eve using

     Q = P(Eve uses Alice’s basis)P(error | Eve uses Alice’s basis)
         + P(Eve uses a different basis)P(error | Eve uses a different basis).      (28)

Determine each probability appearing in this expression.


(d) Detecting the attack and completing the protocol

Alice and Bob estimate the QBER by publicly comparing m randomly selected bits from
their sifted key.
Assuming that errors in different bits are independent, the probability that they observe no
errors is
                          P(no errors) = (1 −Q)m.                             (29)

Therefore, the probability that they detect at least one error is

                       P(at least one error) = 1 −(1 −Q)m.                       (30)

      key.
