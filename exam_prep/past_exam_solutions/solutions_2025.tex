
\subsection{Hyperfine calculation — illustrative, not source-verified}
Previously discussed PHYS4055 revision involved coupling
\(I=\frac52\) and \(J=\frac12\). This is a starter derivation,
not a quotation from, or complete solution to, a verified 2025 question.
The permitted values are \(F=2,3\) and
\[
E_F=\frac A2[F(F+1)-I(I+1)-J(J+1)].
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{E_2=-7A/4,\quad E_3=5A/4,\quad E_3-E_2=3A.}
\]
The degeneracies \(5+7=12\) match
\((2I+1)(2J+1)=6\times2\).
