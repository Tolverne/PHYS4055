
\section{Model potentials, E1 rates and atomic lifetimes}
\textbf{Recognise:} a central model potential gives bound states
whose radial overlap and angular coefficients determine allowed decay.

For \(\psi=R_{nl}(r)Y_{lm}\), first obtain orthonormal
radial eigenfunctions; decide whether software outputs \(R\) or \(u=rR\).

\textbf{Check selection rules} before integrating:
E1 requires opposite parity and the rank-1 triangle/projection rules.
For an allowed transition,
\[
\langle f|\hat{\mathbf d}|i\rangle=-e\langle f|\mathbf r|i\rangle,
\qquad
\int_0^\infty R_f^*(r)\,r^3R_i(r)\,\diff r
\]
is the radial factor; include the angular integral.
Compute
\[
A_{if}=\frac{\omega_{if}^{3}}{3\pi\epsilon_0\hbar c^3}
\big|\langle f|\hat{\mathbf d}|i\rangle\big|^2,
\qquad
\tau_i=\left(\sum_{f:E_f<E_i}A_{if}\right)^{-1}.
\]
With data in atomic units, state the unit conversions.
\(t_{\mathrm{au}}\simeq2.418884\times10^{-17}\,\mathrm{s}\).
\begin{methodbox}
\textbf{Method:} eigenstates \(\to\) allowed E1 channels
\(\to\) angular/radial factors \(\to\) frequencies and rates
\(\to\) sum rates \(\to\) lifetime and measurement comparison.
\end{methodbox}
