====== PAGE 22 ======
Time evolution of a coherent state


Under free evolution, a coherent state remains coherent:
                   α(t) = α(0)e−iΩt

 The state rotates around the origin of phase space.

  Its distance from the origin remains constant.

  Its circular uncertainty region does not change shape.
