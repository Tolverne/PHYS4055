====== PAGE 25 ======
Summary

Conditional measurements produce probabilistic optical operations: the unnormalised output
contains the success probability and is normalised only after the selected detector outcome occurs.

Conditional operations reshape photon-number amplitudes: noiseless amplification
increases the one-photon amplitude relative to vacuum.

Conditional measurement can mimic an optical nonlinearity: the sign-shift operation reverses
the phase of ∣2⟩ while leaving ∣0⟩ and ∣1⟩ unchanged; it is unitary in principle, but its linear-optical
implementation is probabilistic.
