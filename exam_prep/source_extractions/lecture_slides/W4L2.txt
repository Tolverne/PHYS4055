Partial trace

        Where |𝑛)⟩ are the basis                                            Where |𝑛'⟩ are the basis
          vectors for subsystem B        A             B                    vectors for subsystem A

                     •  The operation for obtaining the reduced density matrices of a composite quantum
                system is called the partial trace.
                     •  To obtain the density matrix of subsystem A, we perform the partial trace over
                subsystem B
                     •  To obtain the density matrix of subsystem B, we perform the partial trace over
                subsystem A
                     •  The reduced density matrix for an entangled pure ensemble is a mixed ensemble.
