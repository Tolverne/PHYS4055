(c) Heralded single-photon generation

For small squeezing (λ ≪1), the state approximates:

                         |Ψ⟩≈|0, 0⟩+ λ|1, 1⟩+ O(λ2).

Detecting one photon in the idler mode projects the signal mode onto:

                                      |Ψheralded⟩≈|1⟩s,

up to normalization.  Thus, in the low-gain limit, detection of an idler photon heralds a
single-photon state in the signal mode.
