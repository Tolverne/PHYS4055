====== PAGE 5 ======
From One Atom to Many


 Collective light–matter interactions


          •  The Jaynes–Cummings model describes one two-level atom interacting with one
          quantised cavity mode.

          • We now consider many atoms coupled to the same electromagnetic field.

          • A shared field can make the atoms behave collectively rather than independently.

          •  Collective behaviour can enhance emission, suppress emission, or create states
            that do not couple to the field.

       Jaynes–Cummings −→Tavis–Cummings −→superradiance and subradiance

====== PAGE 25 ======
Summary


              Jaynes–Cummings −→Tavis–Cummings −→superradiance

             •  The Tavis–Cummings model extends atom–field coupling from one atom to an
           ensemble.

             •  Symmetric states couple collectively, producing enhancements such as 𝑔𝑁,
             while antisymmetric states can be dark.

             •  The same collective amplitudes can produce superradiant emission with a peak
             rate proportional to 𝑁2.
