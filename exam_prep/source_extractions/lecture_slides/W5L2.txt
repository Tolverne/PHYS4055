Two descriptions of open-system dynamics

  A localized quantum system interacts with a bath consisting of many
  simple harmonic oscillators, such as free optical modes.

          •  System operator: !𝒄
          •  Bath-mode operators :#𝒃𝝎


  Two equivalent descriptions

     Picture            What evolves?                       Equation of motion
    Heisenberg picture   System operators, such as ̂𝑐(𝑡)       Quantum Langevin equation
     Schrödinger picture   System density operator, !𝜌(𝑡)         Master equation

Both descriptions arise from the same system-bath Hamiltonian and give the same physical predictions.

====== PAGE 25 ======
Summary

 •  The quantum Langevin equation is used to calculate how system operators evolve
   when both damping and environmental fluctuations matter—for example, the field
    inside a lossy optical cavity.

 •  For a thermal white-noise bath, second-order noise products generate the emission
   and absorption terms in the system dynamics.


 •  The quantum Langevin and Lindblad master equations describe the same open-
   system physics from the Heisenberg and Schrödinger pictures, respectively.
