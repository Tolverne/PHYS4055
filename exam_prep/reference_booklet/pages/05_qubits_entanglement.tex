
\section*{Part II. Applications of optics \hfill 5 / 15}
\subsection*{1. A photon can encode a qubit}
A qubit is \(\ket\psi=\alpha\ket0+\beta\ket1\),
\(|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1\). A photon's polarisation
\(\ket H,\ket V\), two paths \(\ket{1,0},\ket{0,1}\),
or two frequency bins can form the logical basis.
On the Bloch sphere,
\(\ket\psi=\cos(\theta/2)\ket0+e^{i\phi}\sin(\theta/2)\ket1\)
up to an unobservable global phase. Pauli operators are
\(X,Y,Z\), and a rotation about axis \(k\) is
\[
\boxed{R_k(\theta)=e^{-i\theta\sigma_k/2}
=\cos(\theta/2)I-i\sin(\theta/2)\sigma_k.}
\]
Wave plates implement polarisation rotations; beam splitters and
phase shifters implement path-qubit operations. Arbitrary
single-qubit unitaries can be decomposed into rotations.

\subsection*{2. Entangled pairs and reduced states}
The Bell states are
\[
\ket{\Phi^\pm}=\frac{\ket{00}\pm\ket{11}}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\ket{\Psi^\pm}=\frac{\ket{01}\pm\ket{10}}{\sqrt2}.
\]
Although \(\ket{\Phi^+}\) is pure,
\[
\rho_A=\operatorname{Tr}_B\bigl(\ket{\Phi^+}\bra{\Phi^+}\bigr)
=\tfrac12 I,\qquad S(\rho_A)=1\ \mathrm{bit}.
\]
Entanglement means the joint state cannot be factored
into a product of states on \(A\) and \(B\).
The reduced state alone does not reveal the correlations.

\begin{methodbox}
\textbf{Partial-trace method:} write
\(\rho_{AB}=\sum_{ijkl}\rho_{ij,kl}\ket{i,j}\bra{k,l}\).
Then \(\operatorname{Tr}_B\rho_{AB}
=\sum_{i,k,j}\rho_{ij,kj}\ket i\bra k\).
For a pure bipartite state, unequal/nonzero Schmidt coefficients
signal entanglement.
\end{methodbox}
\subsection*{3. Producing and characterising correlations}
Spontaneous parametric down-conversion can generate photon pairs
satisfying energy and momentum phase-matching conditions.
Appropriate mode selection and interferometry yield
polarisation/path entanglement. Entropy
\(S(\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}\rho\log_2\rho\) quantifies
a state's mixedness; for a pure bipartite state, subsystem entropy
also measures entanglement. No-cloning forbids a universal
unitary that copies an \emph{unknown} arbitrary state.
